## Problem Statement

Increase the salary of every employee in the Sales department by 5000. Then return every employee (emp_id, emp_name, department, salary) .

The employees table initially contains:

+--------+--------------+------------+--------+
| emp_id | emp_name | department | salary |
+--------+--------------+------------+--------+
| 1 | Aarav Sharma | Sales | 60000 |
| 2 | Priya Nair | Tech | 48000 |
| 3 | Rahul Verma | Sales | 52000 |
+--------+--------------+------------+--------+

### Output
UPDATE 2
 emp_id | emp_name | department | salary 
--------+--------------+------------+--------
 2 | Priya Nair | Tech | 48000
 1 | Aarav Sharma | Sales | 65000
 3 | Rahul Verma | Sales | 57000
(3 rows)

## Solution

```js
UPDATE Sales SET salary=salary+5000;
SELECT emp_id,emp_name,department,salary FROM employees
```
