## Problem Statement

Business scenario

SalesPulse is a B2B distribution company that supplies packaged goods to retail stores across multiple Indian cities. Its sales operations team reviews quarterly performance to determine incentive eligibility and understand how each representative performs relative to their city peers.

The company employs two categories of sales representatives:

 * Full-Time: Must achieve quarterly sales of at least ₹50,000 to qualify for the incentive.

 * Part-Time: Must achieve quarterly sales of at least ₹20,000 to qualify for the incentive.

Each row represents one representative&#39;s total sales for a particular quarter.

Your task

Write a single SQL query to generate the quarterly performance report.

 * Include only representatives who meet the incentive threshold for their staffing type. Exclude staffing types not covered by the programme.

 * Within each city and quarter, assign a position based on quarterly sales in descending order. Representatives with equal sales must receive the same position, and the next distinct sales value must resume after all positions occupied by the tie.

 * For each eligible representative, calculate the average sales of up to two eligible representatives immediately before them in their city&#39;s ordered list for that quarter. Do not include the current representative&#39;s sales in this average.

 * Every eligible representative occupies a separate row in the ordered list, including representatives with tied sales. When sales are tied, use rep_id in ascending order to determine the order of those representatives.

 * If fewer than two eligible representatives precede the current representative, use only the available preceding representatives. If none precede them, return NULL.

 * Round the calculated average to two decimal places.

Output

Return the following columns in this order:

rep_name, city, staffing_type, quarter, quarterly_sales, city_rank, rolling_avg_top2

Sort the final output by city asc, quarter asc, city_rank asc and rep_id asc.

## Solution

```js
WITH eligible_reps AS (
    SELECT
        rep_id,
        rep_name,
        city,
        staffing_type,
        quarter,
        quarterly_sales,
        RANK() OVER (
            PARTITION BY city, quarter
            ORDER BY quarterly_sales DESC
        ) AS city_rank
    FROM sales_reps
    WHERE (staffing_type = 'Full-Time' AND quarterly_sales >= 50000)
       OR (staffing_type = 'Part-Time' AND quarterly_sales >= 20000)
)
SELECT
    rep_name,
    city,
    staffing_type,
    quarter,
    quarterly_sales,
    city_rank,
    ROUND(
        AVG(quarterly_sales) OVER (
            PARTITION BY city, quarter
            ORDER BY quarterly_sales DESC, rep_id ASC
            ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND 1 PRECEDING
        ),
        2
    ) AS rolling_avg_top2
FROM eligible_reps
ORDER BY
    city ASC,
    quarter ASC,
    city_rank ASC,
    rep_id ASC;
```
